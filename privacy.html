Zombie Survivor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Zombie Survivor ("the app") is published by Zahid Ghotia. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data we store

Your progress (missions, stars, coins, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run accounts or servers and we never upload your progress.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Where required, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

Internet access is used only for advertising. Vibration is used for gameplay feedback.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored progress. Questions: zahidghotia@gmail.com
