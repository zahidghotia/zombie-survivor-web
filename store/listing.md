## Full description (4000)
The dead have risen and only you stand between them and the dawn.

Zombie Survivor is a fast, one-hand survival shooter. Move with one finger, your gun fires by itself, and every level up lets you pick a new upgrade. Survive the timer, beat the boss and clear the mission.

SURVIVE THE NIGHT
• 8 handcrafted stages: Graveyard, Village, Dark Forest, Frozen Village, City Streets, Farmlands, Crypt Catacombs and the Haunted Manor
• 80 missions with mini bosses and giant bosses that charge, slam and summon
• Endless Mode on every stage. How long can you last?
• A new Weekly Challenge every week with wild rules

BUILD YOUR POWER
• 4 guns that EVOLVE: Dual Pistols, Minigun, Dragon Breath and the Railgun
• Secondary weapons: Orbit Blades, Gun Drone, Lightning and Grenades
• 12 run upgrades, kill-streak FRENZY mode, dash and hero skills
• Hordes, golden skeletons and supply drops keep every run different

YOUR HEROES
• 5 heroes with unique skills: Rogue, Knight, Barbarian, Ranger and Mage
• Gear with 4 rarities to find, equip and level up
• Permanent hero upgrades, daily rewards, daily tasks, achievements and a season pass

FREE TO PLAY
• No account, no internet required to play, progress saved on your device
• Optional ads give bonuses. Remove ads with one purchase.
• Available in English, Urdu and Hindi

Can you clear all 80 missions with three stars?
